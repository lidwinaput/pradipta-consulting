About

I'm Lidwina Pradipta Putri, a sustainability and human rights professional based in Paris, with working experience in Indonesia and France.

5+ years at IOM - UN Migration in Indonesia (2017-2023) – project management, fundraising, institutional reporting, counter-trafficking programmes including migrant protection assistance, and Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse/safeguarding training.
A consultancy at UNESCO (2023-2024) on the social implications of climate transition policies.

Most recently, a sustainability apprenticeship at Sodexo (2025–2026), a global leader in sustainable food and services — Carbon Disclosure Project coordination (score improvement A– to A), Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions consolidation across 43 countries, and CSRD-aligned climate transition planning.

I work across these sectors deliberately — evidence-based, people-centred, and committed to the principle that sustainability must not come at the cost of the people or planet it claims to serve.
