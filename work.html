What I deliver

Corporate sustainability and accountability

01

Carbon Disclosure Project (CDP) report, start to finish

Questionnaire response, data gathering, internal coordination and submission, run as one cycle. Aligned with the Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures (TCFD) recommendations.

Led the full cycle at Sodexo; score improved from A– to A.

02

Scope 1–3 greenhouse gas inventory across multi-country operations

Data collection from country teams, consolidation, and calculation using recognised emission factor databases (ecoinvent, ADEME, DEFRA).

Supported the consolidation and analysis of Scope 1, 2 and 3 datasets across 43 countries at Sodexo.

03

CSRD-aligned climate transition plan

Transition plan aligned with the Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD): decarbonisation levers, and decarbonisation pathways calculated from available data against a net zero target.

Produced the climate transition plan published in Sodexo's Universal Registration Document.

04

ESG assessment and human rights due diligence

Assessment of sustainability practice for small and medium enterprises. Assessment of labour and human rights risks in operations and supply chains: recruitment practices, migrant worker conditions, and forced labour indicators, structured for the Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive (CSDDD) and environmental, social and governance (ESG) assessments including EcoVadis.

Sustainability assessment for a tour operator, reviewing how its services align with sustainable practice. Environment theme of an EcoVadis assessment. Counter-trafficking and labour migration work at IOM — UN Migration, and trained in the United Nations Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights.

Humanitarian and development practice

05

Programme management and coordination

Implementing a project across government, donor and civil society stakeholders. Available for interim and project-based assignments.

Managed counter-trafficking programmes in Indonesian border regions at IOM — UN Migration, funded by the Australian Department of Home Affairs, the US State Department (Bureau of International Narcotics and Law Enforcement Affairs, and the Office to Monitor and Combat Trafficking in Persons) and the UK Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office.

06

Donor reporting, proposals and grants development

Project reports for donors, governments and UN agencies, including under the United Nations Sustainable Development Cooperation Framework (UNSDCF) and on the UN INFO platform. Institutional data collection, including the IOM institutional questionnaire. Proposals to funds including the Central Emergency Response Fund (CERF) and European Civil Protection and Humanitarian Aid Operations (ECHO).

Fundraising and institutional reporting at IOM — UN Migration.

07

Protection and victim assistance

Case management and referral pathways for people in situations of exploitation, grounded in the Palermo Protocol and its prevention, protection and prosecution (3P) framework. Victim assistance in cases of trafficking and of sexual exploitation and abuse, including accompanying survivors through referral and support.

Migrant protection assistance at IOM — UN Migration: identification, assistance, and return and reintegration support for more than 50 victims.

08

Training, capacity building and advocacy

Training design and facilitation for staff and partners, including on identifying and preventing trafficking in persons and migrant smuggling. Awareness-raising and advocacy campaigns: planning, partner engagement and event coordination.

Designed and delivered Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) and safeguarding training, and training on migration and the Sustainable Development Goals, at IOM — UN Migration. Coordinated the Global Migration Film Festival across Indonesia for four years (2017–2020): 50+ screenings, 5,000+ attendees.

Across both

09

Research and policy analysis

Desk research, literature review and qualitative analysis, written up as a brief or report that can serve as a basis for developing strategy. Open-source research to verify claims and trace information through public records, corporate filings and digital sources.

Consultancy at UNESCO on the social implications of climate transition policies. Master's from Sciences Po Paris with a concentration in research methods, covering qualitative methodology, evaluation of social programmes, and computational media analysis in Python. Trained in open-source intelligence (OSINT) techniques through the Digital Sherlocks programme at the Digital Forensic Research Lab (DFRLab).

10

Data analysis and visualisation

Scattered data from several sources turned into something usable: analysis, visualisation, or a dashboard.

Analysed purchasing and emissions data benchmarked against the EAT-Lancet planetary health diet framework to inform sourcing strategy. MSc in Digital Marketing and Data Analytics at IPAG Business School, with coursework in machine learning, SQL and data manipulation in pandas. Text mining, textual network analysis and social network analysis training at Lembaga Penelitian dan Pengembangan Sosial dan Politik (LPPSP), FISIP Universitas Indonesia.
